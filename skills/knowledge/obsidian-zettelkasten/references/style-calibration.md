# Style calibration

## Eligibility

Direct mode requires all three:

1. At least **10 distinct accepted thought notes** grounded in the user's own explanations.
2. Enough evidence of how the user understands explanations of the requested kind.
3. One trial note explicitly accepted as understandable and representative of the desired style.

Ten is an initial operating threshold, not an empirical guarantee. Until conditions 1–2 hold, use guided mode. Once they hold, propose a trial through the usual approved list. Only acceptance of that trial opens ordinary direct authoring for covered explanatory forms.

Count notes, not revisions, paragraphs or generated examples. Each qualifying note needs an attributable user explanation and explicit acceptance of the resulting formulation. Approval to write a candidate list does not automatically accept its future wording as a style reference. Ask for that feedback when collecting evidence. Explicit batch acceptance may cover identified notes.

AI-generated notes do not count. A formerly generated note qualifies only if the user supplies their own explanatory substance and accepts the result; cosmetic edits or approval alone do not qualify it. Legacy notes with uncertain provenance may inform contextual reading but cannot inflate the threshold.

## Coverage and references

Assess observable preferences: explanation order, depth, introduction of terms, examples, analogies, causal reasoning and treatment of limits/tradeoffs. Domain alone does not determine coverage. Mechanism explanations may transfer across subjects; code examples alone may not establish how to explain competing management interpretations.

Select **3–5 pertinent accepted human-grounded notes** for each direct task, prioritizing explanatory form and actual feedback over keyword overlap. Read those notes, not the entire corpus. When no adequate reference set exists, obtain a guided explanation for the new form; do not reset readiness for already covered forms.

Preserve clarity preferences without copying misconceptions, grammar mistakes or phrasing mechanically. Accuracy and appropriate nuance take precedence. Do not infer personality, intelligence or learning disabilities from writing samples.

## Persistent vault evidence

Read existing agreements/profile first. For a new vault propose `_system/style-profile.md` within the approved scope. It records explicit local evidence, not hidden memory or permanent model training. Scope to the intended user; do not blend learners in a shared vault.

Keep it small:

- Confirmed writing preferences linked to examples.
- Reference table: stable note ID, current path, evidence of the user's explanation, explicit acceptance evidence, explanatory form.
- Readiness: qualifying count, supported forms, gaps, trial note ID/path and acceptance evidence.

Use concise evidence summaries and dates/locators when available. Do not invent quotes or store whole private chats. An explicitly confirmed user-authored sample can establish provenance; ask when unclear. Do not copy the whole note corpus into the profile. Update only after actual feedback changes evidence and include the change in the approved proposal.

Reconcile stale paths by stable ID. Remove deleted/nonqualifying samples from counts; return to guided work if eligibility falls below 10. Materially changed reference content needs reconfirmation before relying on it. A “ready” flag never overrides missing evidence. In shared vaults follow user-specific profiles or ask which learner to use. Keep system files filterable without automatically changing `.obsidian` settings.
